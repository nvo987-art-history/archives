# NVO987 – Culture Visuelle Moderne et Contemporaine

## About

NVO987 – Culture Visuelle Moderne et Contemporaine is a
multidisciplinary artistic and cultural initiative exploring
the intersections of modern and contemporary visual culture,
art history, research and digital identity.

The digital archive infrastructure provides access to
documentary resources, publications, identifiers and
references associated with the initiative.

## Digital Archive

Official archive:
https://archives.nvo987.fr/

The archive serves as a centralized reference point for
online resources and documentary materials associated
with NVO987.
